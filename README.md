- `zar_trades_raw.md` – posted session results from the Telegram channel, Oct 20 – Nov 19 2026 (times in US Pacific)
- `zar_data/` – NQ/ES/YM/NDX price CSVs and example charts
- `zar_research/` – raw source material (transcripts, notes)
