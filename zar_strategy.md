**Goal:** Reconstruct the rules from free public material. The free Telegram channel states “NO SIGNALS EVER” and, in the user’s phrasing, shows only about 80% of the method.

## Overview

Zar’s day-trading model is branded **Razar’s Edge**, with **Anabolic Flow** named alongside it as a related model. The public workflow is top-down:

1. **Daily chart = the tide.** Is it an up day, down day, or inside/neutral day? Is the daily body bullish or bearish? Is the day already extended? Is price trading outside the prior day’s range (“span of travel”)?
2. **30-minute chart = the setup.** Sessions are marked (Asia / London / New York). Support/resistance is mapped as **Zar-zones**. He looks for clean-traffic **turbo zones**, **naked bodies**, zone breaks, impulse/pullback structure, and location versus the **open range** and 9/21 EMAs.
3. **1-minute (sometimes 30-second) chart = execution only.** “I don’t trade the one minute chart.” It is used at the end of the process for timing a buy/sell-stop and for permission (e.g. back below/above the open range).

He trades almost exclusively around the **London and New York opens**, usually in the first ~2 hours of a session (the **optimal trading window**). Typical instrument in current recaps is **NAS100**. He also watches the **“three sisters”** (Nasdaq, S&P 500, Dow) for alignment. Historical videos also show Dow, S&P, gold, and FX, plus a separate **VWAP-reversion** play that is less prominent in the Oct–Nov 2026 recaps.

He does **not** post entries, stops, or targets in advance. Public “trade results” are recaps, usually tagged +2.00R, sometimes +0.5R to +1.5R when “dynamically managed,” and occasionally −1.00R. The pinned “mathematics of 10x” post assumes **1R = 1% of the account**, a **~4.5R/week average** (not a quota), and a Monday recalculation of 1R.

The umbrella idea is: wait for a high-quality setup, risk a fixed R, and take only a handful of trades per week. In a Nov 2026 recap he said he takes **less than one trade a day**.

## Glossary

Each term: Zar’s own wording where we have it, then a short plain-language reading. **INFERRED** means he did not give a crisp definition in the sources reviewed.

### Anabolic Flow

> “This is anabolic flow. A strong up day with a strong bullish body trading above the span of travel of the prior day at the time of entry, of course. Into a clean traffic turbo zone with a naked body.”  
> — [LONDON EARLY TAKE! MAY 22 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMVlkFkkvaU)

> “Strong up days give me permission to do more.”  
> — same video

Also used on strong *down* days. In a short stored-kinetic-energy example: “This is anabolic flow, guys… TCON trade on the 30-minute chart. Anabolic flow back in line with the daily weakness.” ([KINETIC ENERGY PLAY MASTERCLASS!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UrY4v_cu22Q))

**Reading:** A trend-day condition: a strong directional daily body, already trading outside the prior day’s range, moving into a clean turbo zone with a naked body. On those days he allows more aggressive / earlier entries. It is both a market-context tag on recap checklists and a named model (“Razar’s Edge & Anabolic Flow Models” on the membership page).

### Span of travel (of the prior day)

Not given a formal definition, but used as “trading above/below / back into the span of travel of yesterday / the prior day.”  
**INFERRED:** The prior daily candle’s high-to-low range. Being outside it is part of anabolic-flow / trend-day evidence; fading often happens as price comes back into it.

### Turbo zone

> “The fact that we pushed into this clean traffic turbo zone with naked body makes me believe that I would have liked to find a way into that move right there. This is a turbo zone. I love trading turbo zone…”  
> — [LONDON EARLY TAKE! MAY 22 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMVlkFkkvaU)

He repeatedly pairs it with “clean traffic” and “naked body/bodies.” Naked bodies in a turbo zone are also described as **magnets** (“breaking handily back into a turbo zone with naked body up above as a magnet. That naked body, by the way, is about half covered as we speak.” — [MASTERCLASS ON AVOIDING EXTENSION!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9j-Hqmm9Ylg)).

**INFERRED (definition of the zone itself):** A 30-minute area to the left with little prior wick/body clutter (clean traffic) and one or more uncovered large candle bodies that can attract price. Not every mapped Zar-zone is a turbo zone. He also mentions “weak zones” vs strong/rejection zones he will not trade into.

### Clean traffic vs messy traffic

> “If I were to look to the left… and start placing horizontal lines on each of these wicks you will see that I will build quite an infrastructure of messiness… Does this look like clean traffic to you? Well the answer is no. These are places where prices are likely to pause… and get rejected… But above here if I were to place a horizontal line in all of the wicks… you get one here and so I want to see a close above that wick and then there’s nothing else. Clean traffic.”  
> — [FLASH CHAT! CLEAN TRAFFIC EXPLAINED!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cm_AeaMM63Y)

**Reading:** Look left. If many prior wicks/bodies stack at that price, traffic is messy and price is likely to stall. If almost nothing is in the way after a close beyond the last wick, traffic is clean and the move can run.

### Naked body / naked bodies

Used constantly in 2026 recaps (“30M Naked Body,” “Double Naked Bodies,” “Naked Body Magnet”). He also titled an early video [REFRESH YOUR ZONES! GET NAKED!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kjlprYhAfFs), but that video is about stripping a cluttered chart back to a few fresh zones, not a formal definition of “naked body.”

From later usage: he draws markers on uncovered bodies, deletes them when “covered completely,” and treats remaining uncovered bodies as magnets.

**INFERRED:** A relatively large candle body (30-minute or daily) that later price has not yet overlapped/filled. A “naked body magnet” is an unfilled body sitting in (or beyond) a clean-traffic area, which he expects price to be drawn toward.

### Zar-zones (and “GRID” pre-session charts)

> He draws “zones of support and resistance, areas where prices may in fact reject… Get to the top of the bodies… I’ll just draw a little zone…” Session highs/lows, body edges, and sometimes wicks are used. Broken support can be flipped to resistance. Zones that have been “wiped out” are deleted. He maps them before London using Asia (and prior day) structure, then refreshes during the day.  
> — [HOW TO DRAW ZAR-ZONES!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CY130H-PFb4); [REFRESH YOUR ZONES! GET NAKED!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kjlprYhAfFs)

**INFERRED:** The daily “NAS 30M” / “GRID” screenshots in the free Telegram channel are these pre-session 30-minute maps: shaded zones plus directional arrows at clean-traffic areas, without printed prices.

### TCON (Trend Continuation)

Confirmed in his own words, including older X posts:

> “TCON TRADE… LONG DOW - TREND CONTINUATION of the morning impulse wave.”  
> — [X, 18 Jul 2023](https://x.com/ZarillionTrades/status/1681372156356067351)

> A viewer asked why there were no good setups for “a TCON trade or a trend continuation trade.” Zar answered on camera, treating the two phrases as the same thing.  
> — [FLASH! WHY NO TCON TRADES TODAY!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O-wrNLA4Zkw)

> “This is actually my preferred entry on TCON trades, guys. As you probably know, this is my signature trade. These aggressive entries are for clean flowing price action on up days if they’re long trades.”  
> — [LONDON EARLY TAKE! MAY 22 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMVlkFkkvaU)

> “Normally on a trend continuation or TCON trade I like to see an orderly pullback to the 21 or close to it.”  
> — [TCON MASTERCLASS! TIMING THE ENTRY!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JswxAGbsCs8)

**Reading:** His signature setup: continue the current impulse after an orderly pullback (often to the 9 or 21 EMA), rather than fading it. On anabolic-flow days he prefers an **early resumption** entry. On neutral, sluggish, or opposing days he waits for a breakout. “Momentum TCON” / “MOMO TCON” appears when he enters a strong impulse with less of a pullback. A deeper, less orderly pullback can be reclassified as a **Fibonacci TCON / FIB killzone** trade (50–61.8% retracement of the impulse), not a “standard TCON.”

### Open range, 1-minute open range, and Dynamic Open Range

Early (2022) definition:

> “I typically use the 10 minute candle, the first 10 minute candle, as my minimum open range… if prices are inside of this opening range chances are I will not even think about taking a trade, but the real open range needs to be expanded… my actual functional range…”  
> — [FLASH CHAT! OPEN RANGE!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4VctAH-xj8s)

2026 practice is more often built around the **first 30-minute candle** plus nearby pre-market / prior-session highs and lows:

> “My dynamic open range is really just a tool to keep me out of making impulsive decisions based on the lowest time frame. I’m setting an area here, which is basically a no-trade zone, at least for the time being…”  
> “I will often use key pre-market highs and lows as my dynamic open range.”  
> “It’s fine to trade inside of this dynamic open range if a clean setup prints later in the session.”  
> “This break of the 1-minute open range wasn’t an entry signal for me, so I expanded the open range. And this break of the dynamic open range was not an entry signal, so I expanded it again.”  
> — [MASTERCLASS on DYNAMIC OPEN RANGE!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K12mXxKusHU)

**Reading:** Start with a small opening box (historically first 10 minutes; now often first 30 minutes and/or the 1-minute opening range). If early breaks are not clean setups, expand the box (dynamic open range) so he does not trade the ping-pong. Location relative to the open range is a core filter: **longs generally above it, shorts generally below it** (“Shorts below the open range, longs [above the open] range” — [KINETIC ENERGY PLAY MASTERCLASS!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UrY4v_cu22Q)). Checklist items “EMAs Cleared O-Range” and “Above/Below Open Range” are confirmations.

### Waited first 30M / first hour

Recap checklists frequently include “Waited First 30M,” “Waited First 1 Hour,” sometimes 90 minutes or longer. In videos he often refuses to act until the first 30-minute candle of the session closes, especially when the open is choppy.

**INFERRED:** A patience rule, not an absolute ban on the first 30 minutes. On very clean anabolic-flow days he has taken earlier entries. On news days (e.g. FOMC) and manic opens he waits longer.

Stops are wider (often beyond the open range, not just the nearest pivot). Early resumption entries are not used. Entering late into a stretched counter-move is explicitly called a mistake.

### Zone Break

> “Let me just show you my zone break framework… First, we want to identify a period of consolidation or congestion after a move… Then we wait for price to break and close beyond a key 30 minute zone marker… Price must be entering and closing into a clean traffic range… Then I want to enter on a subsequent break of the signal candle… To qualify, the current move should not be overextended… There should be a recent pullback on a lower time frame before entry… You want to seek higher time frame support for the trade idea… be cautious of zone breaks during slow market times of the day.”  
> — [MASTERCLASS ON ZONE BREAK PATIENCE!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aPjfQhONB34)

> “I typically wait for clean break and close into a turbo zone when the picture [is indecisive]…”  
> — [MASTERCLASS BREAK & CLOSE CLINIC!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF9fcvjSkng)

**Reading:** After a move and a coil, wait for a 30-minute *close* through a pre-drawn zone into clean traffic, then enter on a break of that signal candle (not on the first poke). Prefer HTF agreement, a fresh lower-timeframe pullback (often visible as a wick), and the active part of the session.

## Setup types

Common structure for all of them: daily story first, 30-minute setup, buy/sell-stop execution on a 1-minute pivot or candle break, stop beyond structure, target in R (typically 2R when conditions are clean).

### 1. Anabolic Flow + TCON (signature)

- **Context:** Strong up or down day; daily body in the trade direction; price already outside the prior day’s span of travel; clean-traffic turbo zone with a naked body; preferably the three sisters agree; preferably in the optimal window and outside the open range.
- **Trigger:** Impulse, then an orderly pullback (he “likes to see” a pullback to the 21 EMA or close to it). On true freefall / momentum TCON, the pullback may be smaller.
- **Entry:** Preferred: **early resumption** (buy/sell-stop on a shift candle or “current pivot in the making”) *before* a full break of the day’s extreme. Alternative: wait for the breakout. Early entries are for anabolic-flow days only.
- **Stop:** Beyond the shift-candle wick and the current pivot; often also beyond nearby EMAs. Tighter than a Daily Fade stop.
- **Target:** 2R when price action is “very clear”; 1R–1.5R in London or when unclear. All four management models may be shown (see Risk).

### 2. Daily Fade / Rebound / Extreme Extension

- **Context:** A *very* extended daily candle (large selloff or rally), often after multiple days in one direction. Buyers/sellers “disagree” with the prior session (e.g. NY buying an Asia washout). Not used on orderly anabolic-flow days: “these are the types of orderly days that I will not fade unless I have something incredibly compelling” ([LONDON EARLY TAKE! MAY 22 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMVlkFkkvaU)).
- **Trigger:** Signs the extreme is failing (rejected lows/highs, 30-minute body flip, topping/bottoming tail, snapback toward the 9/21 EMA).
- **Entry:** After some confirmation; **not** an early resumption; **not** a late chase of an already-extended counter-move.
- **Stop:** Wider — often beyond the open range, not just the nearest pivot — “enough room to work.”
- **Target:** May still be 2R, but he sometimes accepts 1R / 1.5R / break-even because the stop is larger and the trade is against the daily tide. Higher risk by his own admission.

### 3. Zone Break (and Zone Continuation / Zone Reversal)

- **Context:** A pre-mapped 30-minute zone; a prior move; then congestion. Destination should be clean traffic (ideally a turbo zone). HTF should support the idea. Not overextended. Active session, not the slow lunch period.
- **Trigger:** 30-minute **break and close** beyond the zone marker, into clean traffic.
- **Entry:** Subsequent break of that **signal candle** (he will wait out the rest of a 30-minute bar). Optional lower-timeframe pullback/wick first. “Zone continuation” **INFERRED** as a break that continues an already-established 30-minute swing rather than the first break of a range. “Zone reversal” **INFERRED** as a rejection at a zone then a close back the other way (the 21 Jun 2024 Twilight example was explicitly “a Zone reversal trade” and also a VWAP reversion).
- **Stop:** Beyond the signal structure / prior pivot / the zone, depending on the chart. Same “double or triple protection” idea can apply.
- **Target:** Standard R models; be less ambitious in slow periods.

### 4. Stored Kinetic Energy (late-session resumption)

- **Context:** The optimal window (first ~2 hours) was range-bound / “sleepy,” so the day’s directional energy was not expressed. Daily bias is still clear. He has often already posted “NO TRADES” for that session, then reopens the window.
- **Trigger:** Later expansion — often a break of the dynamic open range and/or session extreme, into a turbo zone, sometimes already in or near the Twilight Zone.
- **Entry:** Sell/buy-stop below/above the dynamic open range or a closing candle; still a TCON-style idea on the 30-minute, in line with the daily.
- **Stop:** Beyond the open range and prior pivots / stacked EMAs (example: “Stop loss above the open range and above prior pivot… and both EMAs”).
- **Target:** Often reduced (1R or 1.5R). He has said he does not need to “track all four models” after the optimal window.

### 5. Open-range V-reversal / break-and-retest of the open range

- **Context:** A drive out of the open, a full or deep V back into or through the open range, then a failure and resume. May coincide with a Daily Fade or with a continuation after a “fake” first move.
- **Trigger / entry / stop:** Stalked on the 1-minute after the 30-minute/daily story is set; entry often on a break back out of the open range, stop beyond the rebound pivot or the far side of the open range. **Exact bar rules are partly INFERRED** from one masterclass plus recap labels.

### 6. Fibonacci TCON (variation)

- **Context:** A TCON-style trend day, but the pullback is too deep/messy for a “standard TCON.”
- **Trigger:** Retracement into the 50–61.8% “kill zone” of the impulse, plus a lower-timeframe shift.
- **Entry / stop / target:** Buy/sell-stop at the shift; stop beyond a nearby wick/pivot. Same R models. He is explicit that this is *not* his standard signature entry.

### 7. Auto-trade (a management style, not a separate pattern)

A Zone Break or similar is armed as a resting order so he can leave. Same setup quality is supposed to apply; he acknowledges the risk that price can “drift down and do all kinds of wonky things and then take me in.”

### 8. Older / less visible in the current 30-day NAS recaps

**VWAP reversion** was a frequent named play in 2022–2025 videos (fade an extension back to session VWAP, sometimes near the Twilight Zone). It still appears in older checklists (“Below VWAP”). **INFERRED:** Not abandoned, but not the headline label in the Oct–Nov 2026 NAS recaps.

## Risk and management

**R definition.** 1R is the dollar amount risked on a trade, usually **1% of account equity**. A +2R target means the profit target is twice the stop distance. He switched from quoting raw points to R-multiples so results are risk-normalized. Source: [R-VALUES & R-MULTIPLES IN TRADING!](https://buymeacoffee.com/zarillionc2/r-values-r-multiples-in-trading); Telegram pinned “Mathematics of 10x” (1R recalculated each Monday; ~4.5R/week is an average, not a quota; example compounding from $10k → $100k in 52 weeks at 4.5%/week).

**Four models shown on many recap videos** (he literally says “all four models”):

1. **1R all-or-nothing** — full exit at +1R.  
2. **1.5R all-or-nothing** — full exit at +1.5R.  
3. **2R all-or-nothing** — full exit at +2R; if price dies first, this model can take a −1R “charge.”  
4. **2R, stop moved to break-even at +1R** — once +1R is touched, the stop goes to entry; result is then either +2R or 0R.

When public posts say **+2.00R WIN**, that is typically the 2R all-or-nothing model filling. **“Dynamically managed”** +0.5R / +1.0R / +1.5R means he lowered the ask (Twilight Zone, FOMC, late stored-kinetic-energy, unclear tape) or took a model that was already done and cancelled the rest. He also writes about moving to break-even “when the circumstances and the environment are right” ([THE POWER OF BREAK-EVEN!](https://buymeacoffee.com/zarillionc2/the-power-of-break-even)).

**Stop craft (“double or triple protection”).** He often stacks invalidation: (1) current pivot, (2) edge of the open range, (3) a key shift candle, “a couple of points or so below that.” Tighter stops make 2R easier to reach but get wicked out more often; he accepts a wider stop when the extra room “saves me often enough.” He only counts a target or stop as hit when price **crosses the line** on his execution platform, not merely tags it on TradingView. Source: [STOP LOSS MASTERCLASS! WIN!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IGnfcfMmt3A).

**Position sizing.** Size so that the chosen stop equals 1R. Exact contract-count workflow is not published.

**Frequency.** Public guidance is 1–3 optimal trades per day, “a handful of optimal trades per week,” and, in one 2026 recap, “less than one trade a day.” Two trades in one session are “fairly rare” and “almost always… in New York versus the London session” when flow is clean (from an earlier “ANOTHER BIG WIN” recap referenced in search; treat frequency by session as **partly INFERRED** from recap habits).

**Important caveat on advertised results.** Because nothing is published *before* the trade, the +2R / 21-of-22 type month in the free channel cannot be independently verified from the posts alone. The videos do show live or hindsight entries with stops and the four models, which is more than the Telegram text, but they are still his selection of what to record.

## Short reconstructed rule-set (for quick reference)

Trade **NAS100** off the **daily + 30-minute**, execute on the **1-minute**. Map **Zar-zones** before London. Default **no trade inside the (dynamic) open range** and often **wait for the first 30-minute close**. Prefer the **first two hours** of London (3:00 a.m. ET) or New York (9:30 a.m. ET). Stand aside in the **Twilight Zone** (~11:30 a.m.–2:00 p.m. ET) unless a rare compelling setup or a **stored-kinetic-energy** release after a dead morning.

**Main play (TCON on an Anabolic Flow day):** strong daily body already outside yesterday’s range, 30-minute impulse into a **clean-traffic turbo zone** with a **naked body**, orderly pullback (often to the 21 EMA), then a resumption entry; stop beyond the pullback/shift; target **2R**. Early entries are allowed only in that condition. Otherwise wait for a breakout, or pass.

**Secondary play (Daily Fade):** only on an *extreme* daily extension; wider stop (often beyond the open range); no early resumption; do not chase a late extended counter-move.

**Secondary play (Zone Break):** coil after a move; 30-minute close through a zone into clean traffic; enter on the next break of that signal candle; skip if already extended or if the session is slow.

**Filters:** inside/tiny days, messy traffic, sisters not aligned, overextension, lunch chop, “the day’s energy already expressed.” Risk **1% per trade**, typical target **2R**, ~**4.5R/week** as an average rather than a quota. Management is often shown as four parallel models (1R, 1.5R, 2R, and 2R with stop to break-even at 1R). A large residual is discretionary: zone drawing, “juice,” and whether he actually clicks the order.
